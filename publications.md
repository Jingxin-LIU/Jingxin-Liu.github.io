## Research Areas

My publications primarily cover:

- **Digital Pathology**: Whole-slide image analysis, tissue segmentation, cell detection
- **Computational Pathology**: Cancer detection, tumor classification, prognostic algorithms  
- **Medical Image Analysis**: Deep learning for histopathology, domain adaptation, multi-modal analysis
- **Computer Vision**: Deep learning architectures, self-supervised learning, explainable AI
