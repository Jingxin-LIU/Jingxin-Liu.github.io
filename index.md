## About

I am an Associate Professor in the School of AI and Advanced Computing at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Nottingham in 2018. My research interests include **Digital Pathology**, **Computational Pathology**, and **Image Processing**, with a strong focus on **Computer Vision** and **Machine Learning**.
