<section class="about-section">
  <h2>About Me</h2>
  <p>I am an <strong>Associate Professor</strong> in the School of AI and Advanced Computing at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University. I received my <strong>Ph.D.</strong> from the University of Nottingham in 2018 under the supervision of Prof. Guoping Qiu, and my <strong>M.S.</strong> degree from the University of Edinburgh in 2013.</p>
  
  <p>My research interests include <strong>Digital Pathology</strong>, <strong>Computational Pathology</strong>, and <strong>Image Processing</strong>, with a strong focus on <strong>Computer Vision</strong> and <strong>Machine Learning</strong>. I am passionate about developing novel AI algorithms to solve real-world challenges in medical imaging and healthcare.</p>
  
  <p>I am also actively building the <a href="https://vibe-lab.example.com" target="_blank"><strong>VIBE Lab</strong></a>, where we focus on cutting-edge research in biomedical image analysis and computer vision. If you are interested in joining our team or collaborating with us, feel free to <a href="mailto:jingxin.liu@xjtlu.edu.cn">reach out</a>.</p>
