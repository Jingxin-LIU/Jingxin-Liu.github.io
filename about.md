  ## Research Interests

  My research focuses on developing novel AI algorithms and deep learning architectures for:

  - **Digital Pathology**: Automated analysis of whole-slide histopathological images
  - **Computational Pathology**: AI-driven cancer detection and classification systems
  - **Medical Image Analysis**: Machine learning techniques for histopathological image processing
  - **Computer Vision**: Deep learning models for medical imaging applications
  - **Biomedical Image Processing**: Advanced algorithms for feature extraction and analysis
