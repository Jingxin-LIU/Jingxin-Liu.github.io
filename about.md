# About Me

I am an Associate Professor in the School of AI and Advanced Computing at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Nottingham in 2018, and my M.S. from the University of Edinburgh in 2013.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on the intersection of:
- **Digital Pathology** & **Computational Pathology**
- **Medical Image Analysis** 
- **Computer Vision** & **Machine Learning**

I am particularly interested in applying artificial intelligence and deep learning to improve diagnostic accuracy and clinical workflow efficiency in digital pathology.

## VIBE Lab

I am building the **Vision Intelligence & Biomedical Exploration Lab (VIBE Lab)** at XJTLU. For more information, visit the [VIBE Lab website](https://vibe-lab.example.com).

I am committed to educating the next generation of researchers in AI and computational pathology. I teach courses including:

- Artificial Intelligence in Medical Imaging
- Computational Pathology Fundamentals
- Machine Learning for Healthcare
- Deep Learning Applications
