## Research Interests

My research focuses on the intersection of artificial intelligence and computational pathology. Specifically, I am interested in:

1. **Deep Learning for Medical Imaging**: Developing convolutional neural networks and other deep learning architectures for automated analysis of histopathological images.

2. **Digital Pathology**: Creating computational frameworks that assist pathologists in cancer detection, classification, and prognostic assessment.

3. **AI in Clinical Practice**: Translating AI models into clinical workflows to improve diagnostic accuracy, reduce turnaround time, and enhance patient outcomes.

4. **Generalization and Robustness**: Addressing challenges in model generalization across different imaging technologies, staining protocols, and patient populations.
